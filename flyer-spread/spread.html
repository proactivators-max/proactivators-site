FOUR HOURS. ONE RESET.

Three personas. Every room. The Sales Rep busy but not productive. The Manager firefighting Q1 instead of building Q2. The Senior Leader respectable but plateaued. This workshop confronts all three.

Hour 01
MASTERING YOUR TIME
The Quadrant Audit — not the corporate poster version. Each person audits their actual week and sees, in numbers, where the hours are going. The illusion of busy gets dismantled in real time.
Hour 02
THE PARADIGM SHIFT
Most performance problems aren't skill problems — they're paradigm problems. We walk the 7 mindset shifts that produce a growth-oriented operator. Each attendee leaves with the 7-Habit Doctrine Card.
Hour 03
THE HABIT LOOP
Cue. Craving. Routine. Reward. We expose the architecture of every habit your team has — then identify the keystones. Each person leaves with a 30-day habit installation plan.
Hour 04
THE MASTERMIND ROUND
Mindset becomes accountability. Tables of five. Ten minutes each. Truth from four people who don't owe you anything except honesty. Closes with a signed 30-Day ProActivator Commitment.
— What You Walk Away With
  • A personal Quadrant Audit showing where your hours actually go

  • A redesigned habit loop for the keystone habit costing you most
  • A signed 30-Day ProActivator Commitment with an in-room partner
  • Follow-up cadence at Day 7, 14, and 30 to install what we built
