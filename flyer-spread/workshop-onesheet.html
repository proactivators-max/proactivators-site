THE EXECUTIVEMINDSET WORKSHOP

A half-day reset that develops your people from the inside out — so it shows up in their work.

— The Premise

Most training tries to change what people do. This changes how they see — because behavior that isn't rooted in a shifted mindset always fades. We put a mirror in front of every person and ask the question that reorders everything: ARE YOU DRIFTING? ARE YOU THRIVING? OR ARE YOU SHIFTING? The shift is the bridge — when identity changes, performance changes with it, at work and in every area of life.

— Who It's For

Your whole team — not just leadership. Sales, service, marketing, technicians, managers. Anyone quietly drifting. Because when a person gets their own life in order — health, focus, family, finances — it shows up in how they show up for your customers.

— The Arc Of The Day
01
The Mindset Shift — the mirror. Drifting, thriving, or shifting? The doorway into a ProActivator identity and the Six Pillars.
02
Habit Formation — how habits really run (cue, craving, routine, reward) at three levels: personal, organizational, societal.
03
The Three P's — Pretender, Producer, Proactive — an honest gut-check on who they are, and how a shift moves them up.
04
The "No More" Moment — every person names the one thing keeping them stuck, and ends it, out loud, in the room.
05
The Mastermind — small groups, real counsel, and a signed commitment with an accountability partner.
— What Your Organization Gets
  • People who manage time + attention with intention
  • A shared language for growth
  • Higher engagement and ownership
  • A culture that confronts drift instead of tolerating it
— The Format

Half-day, in person. Built + proven inside a real operating company. Tailored to your team on a discovery call. Day 7/14/30 follow-up built in so it installs, not fades.
