The Program
proactivatorsclub.com

FOUR HOURS. ONE RESET.

Three people. Every room. The Sales Rep busy but not productive. The Manager firefighting Q1 instead of building Q2. The Senior Leader respectable but plateaued. This workshop confronts all three — with a framework, a workbook, and an in-room accountability partner.

Hour 01
MASTERING YOUR TIME
The Quadrant Audit — not the corporate poster version. You audit your actual week and see, in numbers, where the hours are going. The illusion of busy gets dismantled in real time.
Hour 02
THE PARADIGM SHIFT
Most performance problems aren't skill problems — they're paradigm problems. The 7 mindset shifts that produce a growth-oriented operator. You leave with the 7-Habit Doctrine Card.
Hour 03
THE HABIT LOOP
Cue. Craving. Routine. Reward. We expose the architecture of every habit you have — then identify the keystones. You leave with a 30-day habit installation plan.
Hour 04
THE MASTERMIND ROUND
Mindset becomes accountability. Tables of five. Ten minutes each. Truth from four people who don't owe you anything except honesty. Closes with a signed 30-Day ProActivator Commitment.
— What You Walk Away With
  • A personal Quadrant Audit showing where your hours actually go
  • A 7-Mindset Doctrine Card you reference for the rest of the year
  • A redesigned habit loop for the keystone habit costing you most
  • A signed 30-Day ProActivator Commitment with an in-room partner
  • Follow-up cadence at Day 7, 14, and 30
