The Agenda

FOUR HOURS. ONE RESET.

Hour 01

THE SHIFT

See Where You Are. Decide Where You’re Going.

Before you change what you do, you change what you see. We open with the Quadrant of Time — you audit your real week and watch the illusion of "busy" fall apart in numbers. Then the "Old Lady or Young Woman?" illusion proves a harder truth: most performance problems aren't skill problems — they're paradigm problems. You leave this hour seeing your week — and yourself — with different eyes.

Hour 02

THE PATH

Build the Habits That Build You.

You are what you repeat. We expose how habits actually run — cue, routine, reward — and how an old habit gets reframed instead of white-knuckled. Then we take it across the Six Pillars — Faith, Family, Fitness, Finances, Focus, Fulfillment. In each one you name the habit quietly killing you, and the keystone habit that rebuilds it.

🍴  Lunch & Reset
Hour 03

THE IDENTITY

Who Are You Becoming?

Pretender, Producer, or Proactive. You carry all three — but one of them is running your life right now. The Pretender gives just enough to get by. The Producer carries the weight and makes it work. The Proactive leads — thinks ahead, takes the charge, moves before he's told to. You'll find your dominant one honestly, in the areas that matter. Then we do the only thing that counts: the move toward Proactive. This is the hour the name of this movement gets earned.

Hour 04

THE DECISION

No More. Make It Real.

This is the turn. Everyone writes their one thing — the drift they're done tolerating. Then, out loud, in a room full of people who get it, you say the two words that changed my life in a Miami apartment 22 years ago: No more. And the room says it back. Then you sign — a physical contract naming you the CEO of your own life — and the people at your table sign it with you. Before you commit, I'll warn you about the Wall that's coming, and hand you the kit to survive it. You leave with a signed 30-day commitment and a board of people who notice when you go quiet.

Full refund up to 7 days before the workshop. Inside 7 days, your seat is non-refundable — but you can transfer it to someone else. Email proactivateyourlife@gmail.com if you need to transfer.
What time should I arrive?
Doors open 30 minutes before the workshop. Get there at least 15 minutes early — grab a coffee, settle in, meet a couple of people. We start on time.
What's the dress code?
Business casual or whatever you wear to work. You'll be sitting, standing, and doing partner exercises — comfortable matters more than formal.
What about parking?
Parking details vary by venue. Once your seat is confirmed, you'll get an email with venue-specific instructions including parking, entrance, and check-in.
What if I have a food allergy?
Reply to your confirmation email with any allergies or dietary restrictions and we'll accommodate. Lunch is always a mix — usually with vegetarian and gluten-free options by default.

Yes — each person needs their own seat reservation. The workbooks and table groups depend on knowing exactly who's in the room. Come together, sit together — the tables are round and you pick your own seat.
